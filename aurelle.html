AURÈLLE

A skincare visual identity and campaign presentation shaped by a calm, product-first approach. The direction balances refinement with clear, understated communication.

Creative direction

Quiet, considered and product-led.

The visual direction uses warm neutral tones, refined serif typography and restrained packaging details. Natural-looking product lighting keeps the focus on the skincare range while giving the presentation a soft, composed feel.

The presentation

From identity to campaign.

The work is presented in its original sequence, from the identity board through the campaign applications.
